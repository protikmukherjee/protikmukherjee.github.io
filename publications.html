Architecture-Based Generation of Simulation Models for IoT Twins

Zha, Z., Mukherjee, P., Mustafiz, S.

2026 Annual Modeling and Simulation Conference (ANNSIM '26) · Orlando, FL, USA

Introduces ArchML4IoT, an architecture modelling language and toolchain that bridges the gap between high-level architectural models and the detailed behavioural models needed for executing digital twins. Components are aligned with the IoT Architectural Reference Model; reusable behavioural templates from the STL4IoT library are instantiated automatically to generate a simulation-ready statechart project. Validated using a smart home twin (fire alarm, garage door, lighting).

Engineering Digital Twins with Statecharts: A Smart Home Application

Salma, S., Zha, Z., Mukherjee, P., Mustafiz, S.

ACM/IEEE MODELS 2025 Companion · EDTConf · DOI 10.1109/MODELS-C68889.2025.00037

A model-driven approach for the design and development of digital twins that leverages statecharts for behaviour modelling and code synthesis on both the physical and digital twin. A real-time cloud database enables bidirectional synchronization; dashboard and mobile-app interfaces support monitoring and control. Demonstrated with a smart home application, smart hub of lights, garage-door controller, fire-alarm system, coordinated through a central smart home hub, supporting simulation, reasoning, and run-time adaptation.

Federated Learning for Anomaly Detection in Smart Cities

Mukherjee, P., Khwaja, A. S., Anpalagan, A., Baljon, M.

2025 International Conference on Wireless Communications, Signal Processing and Networking (WiSPNET) · DOI 10.1109/WiSPNET64060.2025.11004967

An improved federated learning framework for anomaly detection in IoT-based smart cities, specifically addressing non-IID data challenges. Combines the FedProx algorithm with transformer and autoencoder backbones to enhance pattern recognition. A direct comparison against FedAvg shows FedProx achieves 86.43% accuracy, outperforming FedAvg on realistic non-IID partitions.

RoadDuAttNet: A Dual-Attention Based U-Net Architecture for Road Extraction from Satellite Image Utilizing Regional Features

Ovi, T. B., Bashree, N., Mukherjee, P., Parthima, M. A.

ICICT4SD 2023 · IEEE · DOI 10.1109/ICICT4SD59951.2023.10303545

A self-supervised attention-mechanism-based U-Net architecture with pyramid feature extraction for road segmentation from high-resolution remote-sensing imagery. CBAM and Squeeze-and-Excitation modules in the encoder; a self-supervised Tri-Level Attention Unit (TAU) in the decoder bridges semantic-information gaps. 99.288% mean accuracy · 80.83% mean IoU · 90.20% mean Dice score, outperforming several state-of-the-art baselines.

Performance Analysis of Various EfficientNet-Based U-Net++ Architecture for Automatic Building Extraction from High-Resolution Satellite Images

Ovi, T. B., Bashree, N., Mukherjee, P., Mosharrof, S., Parthima, M. A.

ICHCSC 2023 · Smart Innovation, Systems and Technologies (SIST), Vol. 376 · Springer · arXiv:2310.06847

Comparative study of EfficientNet variants (B0–B7) as backbones for the U-Net++ segmentation architecture, applied to automatic building extraction from high-resolution remote-sensing imagery. Addresses semantic-information-gap constraints in current deep-learning approaches to building footprint segmentation.
